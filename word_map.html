Word Map

3,000 words, each stored as a list of 48 numbers. Flatten them to a plane and words that behave alike land together. Pick a word to see its neighbours, or solve an analogy by vector arithmetic or with the Analogist.

Every word starts as pure random noise, so the map is a meaningless cloud. Four models train nonstop, in two pairs. An Organizer rearranges the words while a Critic neural net checks that related words really belong together. An Analogist learns to answer analogies while a Judge neural net rules on whether each answer is right. Watch all four improve.
